PRODUCT INFORMATION
LEWATIT® S 1567

Lewatit ® S 1567 is a food grade, strongly acidic cation exchange resin with beads of uniform size
(monodisperse) based an a styrene-divinylbenzene copolymer. Lewatit® S 1567 is manufactured without the
use of solvent.

The monodisperse beads are chemically and osmotically very stable, and they can effectively be disinfected
for the drinking water processing. The optimized kinetics lead to an increased operating capacity compared
to ion exchange resins with heterodisperse bead size distribution.

Lewatit® S 1567 is especially applicable for:

» softening in special systems with regular disinfection
» softening of drinking water

Lewatit® S 1567 is adding special features to the resin bed:

» high exchange flow rates during regeneration and loading
» a good utilization of the total capacity
» a low demand for rinse water
» homogeneous throughput of regenerants, water and solutions; therefore a homogeneous working zone
» nearly linear pressure drop gradient for the whole bed depth; therefore operation with higher bed depth is
possible

The special properties of this product can only be fully utilized if the technology and process used
correspond to the current state-of-the-art. Further advice in this matter can be obtained from Lanxess,
Business Unit Liquid Purification Technologies.

Brightion SFC 90

Descriptions
Brightion SFC 90 is a high purity strong acid cationic exchange resin, gel type. It is mainly designed for
the industrial water softening and demineralization, also can be used in potable water softening.
The high excellent mechanical strength and good ion exchange kinetics makes it a good general-purpose
resin in different applications, compared with other resins in market, this resin is distinguished for its long
service life. It is available in other particle size distribution ranges, and available in Na+ or H+ form.

Subiect: Clarificare responsabilitati in procesul de derulare a contractelor
Salutare,

Pornind de la situatia aparuta in cadrul contractului
YDA – Targu Mures, este necesara clarificarea responsabilitatilor fiecarui departament in procesul de derulare a contractelor, astfel incat sa evitam blocajele operationale.

In acest caz, dupa incasarea integrala a contractului si sosirea echipamentelor in depozit, livrarea nu poate fi programata deoarece exista o neclaritate privind gestionarea biletului la ordin.

Contractul prevede ca biletul la ordin se preda Beneficiarului odata cu prima factura de avans si se restituie Furnizorului dupa livrarea echipamentelor.

Responsabilitatile trebuie delimitate pe acelasi principiu aplicat si celorlalte documente generate in cadrul procesului:

Sales gestioneaza partea comerciala: oferta, negocierea, confirmarea comenzii, contractul si predarea dosarului catre departamentele implicate.

Facturarea gestioneaza documentele financiare si contractuale aferente procesului de facturare: emiterea facturilor, urmarirea incasarilor, gestionarea biletelor la ordin si
a celorlalte garantii financiare, precum si recuperarea acestora atunci cand contractul prevede acest lucru.

Logistica gestioneaza livrarea si transmite documentele aferente transportului, respectiv avizul de expeditie si AWB-ul, dupa confirmarea ca toate conditiile comerciale si
financiare sunt indeplinite.

Fiecare departament transmite catre client documentele pe care le genereaza si isi asuma responsabilitatea pentru procesul pe care il gestioneaza.

In forma actuala, anumite activitati si documente nu au un responsabil clar, ceea ce conduce la interpretari diferite intre departamente si poate bloca livrarea unor comenzi, desi toate conditiile contractuale
sunt indeplinite.

Este necesara stabilirea unui flux unic de lucru, cu responsabilitati clar definite pentru fiecare departament, astfel incat astfel de situatii sa nu mai apara pe viitor.

Multumesc.
