SAFETY

Wet activated carbon adsorbs oxygen from the air, what causes danger while

Sorbotech® GE 604 is an extruded activated carbon manufactured
from selected grades of coal. Its high developed surface area allows
effective adsorption of a wide range of organic compounds present
in gases or air streams. Its perfectly available pore distribution makes
it an excellent medium for industrial applications and ventilation
systems.

Packaging

SAFETY

Wet activated carbon adsorbs oxygen from the air, what causes danger while
working inside containers with activated carbon in closed or poorly ventilated
places.

Under certain conditions, during contact with oxidizing agents and some organic
compounds such as ketones (e.g. acetone), acrylates, esters, volatile acids,
amines etc. activated carbon may react exothermally. In case of rapid
temperature increase cooling with water is recommended.

We are not able to design and engineer the complete ozone treatment process, but we would like to give you some recommendations:

If there is no residual ozone in the water allowed at the consumption point you have to destruct it.

UV lamps (254 nm) with higher dosage (e.g. 850 J/m2) are often used for this application. Approx 0,2 ppm of ozone in the water in front of such a lamp will convert to 0 ppm after the lamp.

Of course, to be sure a measurement should be installed.

On the drawing it is not clear how the tanks are connected.

Ozone could be injected e.g. in a tank circulation of the first tank. The ozonized water of this tank flows further to the other tanks where the ozone concentration decreases so that only a rest of ozone (if any) must be destructed by UV.

At the tank with ozone injection the highest ozone concentration in the gas phase must be expected. The ozone gas that has not dissolved in the water escapes into the atmosphere and leaves the tank via the “breathing pipe”. This gas is toxic and corrosive and must be destroyed by an ozone destructor. The catalyst material converts ozone back into oxygen.

Size of destructor depends on the total gas flow rate at the “breathing pipe”.

Please find enclosed sketch of the principle “first tank ozone circulation”.

A measurement device between pump and injector can control the ozone generator by 4 – 20 mA signal.

If the water residence time in the other tanks becomes too long, recirculating the water to the first tank could be an option.
